A studio built around the wheel

SoilArt began in a garden shed with one second-hand wheel and a lot of broken mugs.

Our story

Sonal Jadhav started throwing pots in 2012 after a night class turned into a daily habit. Within two years the shed was too small, and the studio moved into the old foundry on Kiln Lane.

Today six makers share the space, two kilns and a glaze library of more than 200 tests. We still sell mostly to people who stop by, and we still name every glaze after the weather.

What we care about
